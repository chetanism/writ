# Risks

> **Role:** every risk to the product or its delivery, one family, one file. The `Scope` column
> says which — `product`, or the milestone it threatens — so a delivery risk is not a second
> family in a second document. **A register:** one table. A risk is *mitigated* or *accepted*,
> never satisfied, so the family is not traceable.
> **Owner:** <the product owner> for product risks, <the slicer> for delivery risks.
